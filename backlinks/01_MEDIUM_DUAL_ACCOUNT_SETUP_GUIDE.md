## 🛡️ Step-by-Step Publishing Checklist (With 100% Free Canonical Tags)

Setting canonical tags is **completely free** on basic Medium accounts:

1. Click **Write** on whichever account you're publishing from.
2. Copy the **Title** and **Body Content** from [`RANKUR_MEDIUM_POSTS.md`](file:///e:/FREELANCE%20WEBSITE/prototype/RANKUR_MEDIUM_POSTS.md).
3. Insert the matched cover image from [`medium posts/`](file:///e:/FREELANCE%20WEBSITE/prototype/medium%20posts/) using the Camera icon.
4. Click the **three dots (`...`)** at top right $\rightarrow$ **More settings** $\rightarrow$ **Advanced Settings**.
5. Check **"This story was originally published elsewhere"**.
6. Paste the exact URL: `https://rankursite.com/blog/[slug]`.
7. Click **Publish** $\rightarrow$ add the 5 specified tags $\rightarrow$ click **Publish now**.
