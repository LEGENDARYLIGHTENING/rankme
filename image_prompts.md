## 7. How to Use in Google AI Flow

1. **Select Model:** Choose **Imagen 3** (or highest fidelity mode in Google AI Flow / Gemini).
2. **Aspect Ratio:** Set the aspect ratio matching the tag above (`16:9` for banners, `4:3` for cards, `1:1` for logos/avatars).
3. **Negative Prompts (Add to settings):**
   ```text
   blurry, low quality, cartoon, childish, generic clip-art, distorted fingers, watermarks, stock photo handshake, oversaturated neon, cluttered, chaotic, low-res
   ```
4. **Save Location:** Save generated `.webp` or `.png` images directly into `prototype/public/` with descriptive names (e.g. `public/hero-dashboard.webp`, `public/saas-pipeline.webp`).
