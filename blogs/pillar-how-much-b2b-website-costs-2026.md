# How Much a Custom B2B Website Costs in 2026

If you're asking how much a website costs in 2026, you're likely getting wildly different answers. You might have a freelancer quoting you $2,500, a local agency quoting $15,000, and an enterprise dev shop suggesting $80,000. 

The confusion stems from the fact that these three vendors are selling you entirely different products. 

Here is the unfiltered reality of what B2B websites cost today, what you're actually paying for, and why cheaping out is often the most expensive mistake a founder can make.

## The Three Tiers of B2B Websites

### 1. The Digital Brochure ($2,000 - $5,000)
**Built on:** WordPress, Squarespace, or generic Webflow templates.
**The Deliverable:** A visually acceptable 5-to-10 page site that lists your services, shows some stock photos, and has a basic contact form. 
**The ROI:** Zero. These sites do not generate leads. They exist purely so that when someone already knows your name and Googles you, they confirm you have a pulse. You are paying for a digital business card.

### 2. The Agency Standard ($8,000 - $20,000)
**Built on:** Custom WordPress, Webflow, or basic headless setups.
**The Deliverable:** Custom UI/UX design, minor SEO optimization, decent copywriting, and integration with your CRM (HubSpot/Salesforce). 
**The ROI:** Moderate. If you already have strong traffic, these sites will convert better than a digital brochure. However, they lack the underlying programmatic architecture required to actively *hunt* and capture new search share at scale.

### 3. The Programmatic Growth Engine ($15,000 - $40,000+)
**Built on:** Next.js (React), Vercel Edge Networks, Headless CMS.
**The Deliverable:** This is what we build at [Rankur](/). It is not a website; it is an infrastructure. It includes programmatic SEO capabilities (allowing you to launch hundreds of localized, high-intent landing pages instantly), millisecond load times, enterprise-grade security, and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) for ChatGPT and Perplexity.
**The ROI:** Pipeline dominance. These systems are designed to actively pull high-value B2B leads from search and AI platforms month after month. 

## Why You Shouldn't Buy a "Website"

The biggest mistake B2B founders make is treating their website like a capital expenditure rather than a sales asset. 

If you spend $3,000 on a basic WordPress site that generates zero leads over two years, that site didn't cost you $3,000. It cost you hundreds of thousands of dollars in missed pipeline. 

Conversely, if you invest $20,000 into a custom React infrastructure that generates just *two* new enterprise contracts a month, the site pays for itself in weeks. 

## The Hidden Costs of Legacy Tech

When pricing out a project, founders rarely factor in the hidden costs of legacy platforms like WordPress:
*   **Security vulnerabilities:** Constant plugin updates and hacking risks.
*   **Performance decay:** The more plugins you add, the slower the site gets, directly harming your Google rankings.
*   **Developer hostage situations:** Customizing legacy PHP requires constant developer retainers just to keep the site functioning.
