## Part 3: How Clutch Reviews Work (Crucial Rules)

Clutch has strict review verification. If a review looks fake or automated, Clutch will reject it. Here is how Clutch reviews actually work:

### The 2 Methods of Review on Clutch
1. **Online Review Form (Best for Projects under $25,000):**
   * You send your client/friend your unique Clutch review link.
   * The reviewer **must sign in using LinkedIn** or a business domain email. Personal Gmail accounts can work if their LinkedIn profile is attached.
   * They answer 4 structured questions about the project (takes 4 to 5 minutes).
   * A Clutch editor manually reviews and approves it within 48 to 72 hours.
2. **Phone Interview (For Projects over $25,000):**
   * Clutch schedules a 15-minute phone call with the client to verify high-ticket work. (Stick to the online form for now).

### What Clutch Reviewers Are Asked
Clutch does not ask for just a single paragraph. They ask 4 specific questions:
1. **Background & Scope:** What was the project scope and business challenge?
2. **Results & Metrics:** What measurable outcomes or improvements were achieved?
3. **Project Management:** How did the team communicate and meet deadlines?
4. **Strengths & Areas of Improvement:** What was most impressive, and what could be improved?

## Part 6: Best Practices for Fast Verification

1. **Reviewer Identity:** Ensure your reviewers authenticate using their active LinkedIn accounts. This guarantees immediate verification on both Clutch and GoodFirms.
2. **Spacing Out Reviews:** Do not submit 10 reviews in one day. Aim for **1 verified review per week** on Clutch and **1 review every two weeks** on GoodFirms.
3. **Target Milestone:**
   * **Clutch:** 3 verified reviews unlocks your "Verified Agency" badge and a direct do-follow profile link.
   * **GoodFirms:** 2 to 3 reviews places you on the first page of city and category filters for boutique web developers.
4. **Linking Back to Rankur:** Once your profiles are approved, add their profile badges or links to your website footer or About page to close the entity loop.
